
# Recommendation: Display Discount Expiration Date on Products

## 1. Problem Description

Currently, when a product is on sale, customers can see the original and discounted prices, but there is no information about how long the discount will remain available.

The absence of an expiration date prevents customers from planning their purchases effectively and making fully informed decisions. Customers may also feel uncertain about whether they should purchase the product immediately, as they do not know when the discount will end.

## 2. Recommendation

It is recommended to display the discount expiration date or a countdown timer on discounted product pages.

### Examples:
- "This discount is valid until November 30."
- "2 days and 5 hours remaining until the discount ends."

## 3. Expected Benefits

- Improve transparency regarding promotional offers.
- Help customers make informed purchasing decisions.
- Allow customers to plan their purchases.
- Improve the overall shopping experience.
