# REC-MIC-001 – Visual Alert When Speaking While Microphone Is Muted

* **Type:** Improvement / Feature Request (UX / Usability)
* **Priority:** Medium
* **Context:** Live Sessions & Debates (Web / Mobile)

## Problem
During a live session or debate, a user may start speaking while their microphone is muted (and potentially with their camera off as well) without noticing. When this happens, other participants may not be able to alert them in time, and the interface does not provide any visual cue or prompt indicating that speech/audio activity is detected while the microphone is muted.

## Recommendation
When the microphone is muted and audio/speech activity is detected, display a clear yet unobtrusive visual indicator or prompt on the user's tile/card or screen—such as a highlighted or pulsing ring/animation and a brief message like *"Your mic is muted — turn it on to speak"*.

## Expected Benefit
Reduces awkwardness and lost time during live sessions and helps users quickly notice that their microphone needs to be turned on before speaking.
